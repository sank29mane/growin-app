# Plan 49.2: ANE-Specific Re-export for Sub-Millisecond Dispatch

## Objective
Re-export the `NeuralJMCE.mlpackage` model targeting `CPU_AND_NE` specifically. This addresses the gap identified in Plan 49.01 where the model fell back to CPU due to being compiled for `MLComputeUnits.all` (Metal GPU). Compiling with `CPU_AND_NE` and float16 precision ensures actual dispatch to the Apple Neural Engine (ANE) to hit the 3ms latency threshold.
