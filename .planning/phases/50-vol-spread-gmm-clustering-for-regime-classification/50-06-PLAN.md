# Phase 50-06 Plan: Live Integration & Profitability Harness

**Goal**: Connect the tick ingestion, Welford standardizer, Numba GMM inference, and MLX adapter manager into the primary trading loop.

## Requirements
- End-to-end latency from market tick to LLM inference readiness must not exceed 2.5ms.
- High profitability constraints: position sizing dynamically scales based on the GMM regime confidence (e.g., lower leverage in Regime 2 Crisis).

## Steps
1. Update `backend/trading_loop.py` to ingest tick data into the online Welford standardizer.
2. Call the Numba JIT GMM inference with the normalized features.
3. Determine the dominant regime and execute the MLX adapter hot-swap if a regime shift is detected.
4. Dynamically adjust the trade risk limit parameters based on regime probabilities.
5. Run a full 10k iteration dry-run benchmark to verify stability, latency, and simulated profitability metrics.
